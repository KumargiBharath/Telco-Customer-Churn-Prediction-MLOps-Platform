1. Overview
This document outlines the problem definition for the production-grade ML system designed to predict customer churn for a Telecommunications service provider.

2. Problem Statement
The business is losing revenue due to customer attrition. Current retention strategies are reactive and lack personalization. We need a system to predict churn probability for each customer in real-time.

3. Objectives

3.1 Business Objective
Goal: Reduce monthly churn rate by identifying at-risk customers early.
Action: Trigger targeted retention workflows (e.g., discounts, customer support outreach) for customers flagged as churn=1.
KPI: Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) retention and Campaign ROI.

3.2 Machine Learning Objective
Task: Binary Classification (churn vs not churn).
Optimization Target: Maximize Recall to capture maximum churners.
Constraint: Maintain Precision above a usable threshold to avoid wasting marketing resources.
Output: Prediction label (0/1) and Probability Score (0.0 to 1.0).

4. Data Strategy
Source: Customer Demographics, Services Signed Up, Account History.
Target: Churn column.
Frequency: Model retraining schedule (TBD based on drift analysis).

5. Stakeholders
Product: Define retention offers.
Data Science: Build and maintain the model.
Engineering: Deploy API and monitoring infrastructure.
